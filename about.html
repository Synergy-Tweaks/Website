About Synergy Tweaks

We build open Windows tools for people who want a cleaner, more controllable system — without the marketing noise.

What we build

SynergyOS is our flagship project: an AME Wizard .apbx playbook that debloats and hardens a clean Windows install, with options you choose during setup.

SynToolkit is the WinUI 3 companion app for Windows 10 and Windows 11. It includes a live hardware dashboard, tweak controls, and related utilities, and is licensed under GPL-3.0.

All source is public under the Synergy-Tweaks organization on GitHub.

How we work

Synergy Tweaks is volunteer work. There is no company payroll behind these projects — Kwanteks, Bry1k, and our contributors put in their own time after school, work, and everything else life demands.

We ship when we can, answer questions when we can, and keep the source open so others can help. That slower pace is intentional: we would rather do careful, honest work than rush features we cannot support.
