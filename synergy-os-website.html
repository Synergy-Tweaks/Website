What Is Synergy

Not a script.
A whole new OS.

Synergy isn't just another debloat script. It's a fully reworked Windows experience — refined, tested, and tuned from the ground up for those who refuse to accept a slow, bloated, spying operating system.

Built on Windows 11, Synergy strips out telemetry, ad frameworks, unnecessary services, and background noise — leaving you with an OS that does exactly what you tell it to do.

Applied as a playbook over your existing Windows install. No fresh install. No losing your apps or data. Just better.
